Key findings
PPR beat plain TACM: MRR 0.189 vs 0.170 (+11%), Hit@5 32% vs 28%, paired p=0.054 — marginally significant.
PPR also trims tokens (5193→4981) at similar latency (2.8s vs 3.7s).
Hybrid (RRF of bm25+minilm) wins MRR outright at 0.222, p=0.033 vs bm25. It's the real bar to beat.
Hybrid-cs roughly ties tacm-ppr (0.187 vs 0.189) but at half the latency.
BM25 is near the top on this dataset — SWE-bench queries have strong lexical overlap with GT.
Miss taxonomy: 64–68% of misses are ranked_outside_k (in pool but rank>5), 4% gt_not_in_pool (graph-build issue on 2 sympy instances). No miss_other.
Takeaway
PPR is a clean win over vanilla TACM (+11% MRR, p=0.054) with 4% lower token cost. But TACM (even with PPR) still trails Hybrid on this benchmark — the BM25+MiniLM RRF fusion beats structural reasoning here. To reach #1, TACM-v2 needs a component that captures what Hybrid has: dense-semantic signal. Without ML models, that means engineering better lexical expansion (better BM25 variants, richer identifier tokenization) rather than adding a dense encoder.
